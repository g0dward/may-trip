Bamford Edge — out and back

~2-3 miles ~150m climb ~1-1.5 hours The Ladybower view
P Primary parking: New Road layby (free, 6-8 spaces, fills early)
P2 Backup parking: Bamford village pay-and-display
1 Path up from New Road
2 Bamford Edge — the iconic Ladybower view
3 Continue along the edge (turn around when ready)
Path is impossible to lose. Steep up through fields/bracken from the layby to the edge, then walk along the gritstone clifftop with Ladybower Reservoir below for as far as you fancy. Turn around when you've had enough — same way back. Pure out-and-back.

Turn-by-turn

P
0 mi · Start (New Road layby)

New Road / Hurstclough Lane above Bamford. Free layby, only 6-8 spaces — fills by 9-10am on dry weekends. If full, drop down 5 min into Bamford village and use the pay-and-display car park instead (S33 0BN).

1
0.3 mi · 10-15 min · 100m steep climb

From the layby, take the signed footpath through fields and bracken up onto the edge. Steep but short — one functional climb. Pace it.

2
~0.6 mi · 15-20 min along the edge

Top out on Bamford Edge. Walk north-east along the gritstone clifftop. The iconic Ladybower view opens up after a few hundred metres — the photographed angle, reservoir snaking through the valley below. Lots of natural seating on the rocks for a snack.

3
~1 mi · 20-30 min · or turn around earlier

Continue along the edge as far as you fancy — it runs for about a mile of clifftop, view the whole way. Turn around whenever. Walk the same way back.

P
~2-3 mi · ~1-1.5 hrs total · home

Same route down. Steep descent at the end, watch your knees. Back at the car, drive south on A6013 → A57 → M1 → home.
