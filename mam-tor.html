Option A · easier

Ridge out-and-back, drive to lunch

~4-5 mi ~200m climb ~2-2.5 hrs walking

Up Mam Tor, ridge to Lose Hill (or earlier), back the same way to the car. Drive 5 min down Winnats Pass to Castleton for lunch at the Bull's Head.

Castleton parking: Bull's Head has a small car park, or use Castleton main pay-and-display (Cross Street, signed, 2 min walk to the pub).

Option B · full loop

Ridge to Castleton, back via the broken road

~5.5-6 mi ~350m climb ~3 hrs walking

Up Mam Tor, ridge all the way to Lose Hill, drop south into Castleton (250m descent), lunch at the Bull's Head, then climb back to Mam Nick via the abandoned A625 — Mam Tor's "broken road" past the landslip face. ~150m climb back from Castleton.

No driving needed — the loop returns you to the car. But it's harder than yesterday's walk (which you rated Hard).
