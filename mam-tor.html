Tue 5 May 2026 · Walk 1 of the week

Mam Tor + Great Ridge (out and back)

~4-5 miles ~200m climb ~2-2.5 hrs Drive to Castleton for lunch
P Start & end: Mam Nick NT car park
1 Mam Tor summit
2 Hollins Cross
3 Back Tor
T Turnaround: Lose Hill (or earlier if tired)
L Lunch: Bull's Head, Castleton (drive 5 min from car park)
Path is impossible to lose. The Great Ridge is a single spine path with a stone wall to follow. Turn around whenever you've had enough — Hollins Cross, Back Tor, or all the way to Lose Hill. Walk back to the car the same way (mostly downhill on the return), then drive 5 min down to Castleton for lunch at the Bull's Head.

Turn-by-turn

P
0 mi · Start

Mam Nick NT car park at the top of the pass between Castleton and Edale. Pay-and-display. Postcode area S33 8WB. Backup: Edale village car park.

1
0.3 mi · 10 min · Climb 100m (the only real climb)

Cross the road from the car park to the path on the north side. Follow the paved path up to Mam Tor summit (517m, paved trig). Big views begin here.

2
1.4 mi · 30 min · Gentle ridge

From Mam Tor, head east along the Great Ridge. Single obvious spine path with a stone wall. Drop a little, then arrive at Hollins Cross (the saddle, junction with paths down to Edale and Castleton). Solid turnaround point if you're feeling tired.

3
2.1 mi · 20 min · Short scrambly bit

Continue east along the ridge. Slight rise to Back Tor — short scrambly climb up the rocky step (the ridge's drama bit). Another good turnaround point.

T
2.8 mi · 20 min · Final ridge push

Final push along the ridge to Lose Hill summit (476m, trig). End of the ridge. Turn around here (or earlier — Hollins Cross and Back Tor are both legitimate stop points).

←
Return · same way · mostly easy/downhill

Walk the ridge back to Mam Tor, then down to the car. The descent off Mam Tor at the end is steep but short. From Lose Hill that's about 2.8 miles back; from Hollins Cross it's about 1.4 miles back.

L
Drive 5 min · Castleton

Back at the car, drive down to Castleton via the A6187 (Winnats Pass road, very scenic). Park at Castleton main car park. Bull's Head is in the village centre, walk-in fine on a Tuesday. Or Three Roofs Café / The George if it's late.
