Wed 6 May 2026 · afternoon ride

Sheldon + Magpie Mine + Ashford loop

~10-12 miles ~150m climb (Sheldon) ~2 hours All lanes
P Start & end: Little Longstone (Packhorse Inn)
1 Great Longstone
2 Ashford-in-the-Water (Sheepwash Bridge)
3 Sheldon (climb up to)
4 Magpie Mine (lead mine ruin)
5 Lanes back via the limestone fields
Rough route — the lanes are obvious once you're on them. Sheldon is the only real climb (after Ashford); ebike on full assist eats it. From Magpie Mine, take the lane east back toward Ashford or loop further north on the back lanes if you want extra mileage. Keep an eye on time so you're back by ~6pm to rest before dinner.
P
0 mi · Start

Out of the Packhorse, through Little Longstone east on the lane.

1
~1 mi · gentle

Through Great Longstone. Continue south on the lane.

2
~3 mi · gentle descent

Drop down to Ashford-in-the-Water. Sheepwash Bridge (15th century) for a quick photo. Then turn west out of the village.

3
~5 mi · 150m climb · the only real ascent

Climb west up the lane to Sheldon — handsome stone village on the high plateau, less-trodden, big sky. Stay seated, full assist, low gear. Spin up.

4
~6 mi · short detour

Out of Sheldon south-west, follow the lane to the bridleway sign for Magpie Mine. The ruin sits just off the lane in open moorland — best-preserved lead mine in the Peaks, photogenic, big-sky views all around.

5
~7-10 mi · rolling lanes

Back lanes through the limestone fields toward Monyash road, loop back east via the quiet network of lanes through Kirkdale / Little Shacklow. Pick whichever lanes look good — they all eventually drop back toward Ashford or Hassop.

P
~10-12 mi · home

Back through Ashford or Hassop, north through Great Longstone to Little Longstone. Done.
